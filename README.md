F.A.Q:
Q: Where are the settings?
A: In the Vanilla Expanded Framework mod options, under the "Toggleable patches" page.

Q: How can I change my yautja's crest pattern color?
A: In the Big and Small mod options, under the "Extras" tab, enable "show Color Palette button". There will now be a button adjacent to their faction, ideology, and favorite color. Click it, navigate to "tagged", and enable it. For HAR yautje of the xenotype yautja, they do not have this ability due to their head not being a gene. If you add the "crested head" gene to them this should be rectified.

Q: Will you be making this Combat Extended compatible?
A: Aqued made a CE patch that you can find here!

Q: How do I get my yautja to wear the gauntlets?
A: Gauntlets aren't a piece of equipment-- they are implants. They need to be installed, and removed, with surgery. Don't worry though, these surgeries have a custom success chance of 100% with no death % and no need for medicine!

Q: I have the gauntlet marked as a surgery bill but none of my colonists will install it. What gives?
A: Unless the setting for "any race can install gauntlets" is marked as true only yautja can perform the surgery to install gauntlets.
